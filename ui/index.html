    Wiretok v0.2 Setup Guide

    Step 1 / 4

    1. Local Tools 2. Privacy & Era 3. Billing Plans 4. HUD & Mobile QR

    1. Local Tools & 1-Click Sync

    Wiretok scans Antigravity 2.0, OmniRoute, 9Router, Claude Code, Codex, and OpenCode locally without ever reading OAuth tokens, cookies, or credentials.

    2. Privacy Mask & Era Filter

    Email addresses (a***e@example.com) and user home paths are masked by default for safe screen sharing. Toggle Privacy: On/Off in the top bar anytime.

    Since Milestone (Live) tracks fresh sessions after setup; All History includes historical archives.

    3. Account Billing (API $0 vs Subscription)

    Auto-discovered accounts start on API ($0 fixed fee) so no fake monthly subscription charges inflate your totals.

    Switch any account with 1 click (API | Sub | Free) on the Accounts tab to compare API List Value, Actual Paid, and List Value Multiplier accurately.

    4. Turbo Mini-HUD, Sidecar & Mobile LAN QR

    Mini-HUD opens a compact 340×112 always-on-top capsule showing live context %, tokens remaining before /compact, tok/s, and 1-click /compact copy.

    Mobile QR opens an opt-in 6-digit PIN protected read-only view on your local Wi-Fi.

    Mobile LAN QR Pairing (PIN Protected)

    Read-only live dashboard access from your phone over local Wi-Fi/LAN. All write, proxy, and integration endpoints stay locked to localhost and privacy masking is enforced.
